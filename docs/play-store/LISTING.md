**Full description:**
JugglucoTBR is a minimalist AMOLED watch face that puts your glucose next to a large, outdoor-readable clock.

* Large hh:mm clock, glucose with trend arrow, reading age
* 4 customisable complications (battery, steps, weather, calendar by default)
* Always-on display shows only time and glucose
* 8 colour themes
* Battery friendly: pure Watch Face Format, no background service

Requires Juggluco installed on the watch (or its Wear OS complication providers) to display glucose.
Not a medical device. Do not use for treatment decisions.
